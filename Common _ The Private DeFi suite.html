FAQ

  • What is Common Web App?

    Common Web App is a dapp that allows users to break the traceability of their crypto activity — without using centralized exchanges. In just a few clicks, anyone can connect any wallet, deposit into a private shielder contract, and withdraw to a new, unlinkable wallet address.

  • What is Common Mobile?

    Common Mobile is a privacy-first self-custodial crypto app that lets you send, spend, and earn crypto securely—without exposing your financial data.

  • Is Common Mobile App a wallet?

    Not exactly. Common is more than just a wallet—it’s an all-in-one app for privately managing on-chain finance—from your transactions to yield earning and seamless crypto spending.

  • Do I need KYC to use Common Apps?

    No, you can use Common without KYC for core features. However, some fiat on/off-ramp services may require verification. You can also plug into Common with your existing crypto wallet.

  • Are Common Apps safe to use?

    Yes! Common Apps have been audited by Kudelski Security, a leading cybersecurity firm, and by zkSecurity, a team of cryptography experts, to ensure your assets and data remain safe.

  • How does Common protect my privacy?

    Common uses Zero-Knowledge (ZK) technology to shield your balances, transactions, and portfolio data. This ensures your financial activity stays private by default—hackers, data harvesters, and surveillance entities are stopped cold from viewing your crypto activity.

  • Which blockchains and tokens does Common support?

    Common currently supports Aleph Zero EVM and Arbitrum, with more chains coming soon.

  • How do I get started on Common Web?

    Simply go to Common Web, connect your wallet of choice, and make your first deposit into the Shielder.
